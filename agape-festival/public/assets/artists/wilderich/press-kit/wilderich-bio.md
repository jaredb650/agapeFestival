Since infancy, music has been his refuge, shaped by his parents' diverse selections. Despite academic struggles, he found solace and purpose in music, embarking on a journey of self-discovery. Learning piano by ear and through online resources, he honed his skills and expanded into songwriting and production. Immersed in electronic genres like Dubstep and DnB, he gravitated towards DJing and producing, drawn to the emotive depth of artists like Funk Tribu and Eargasm God. Over time, he cultivated his signature style, blending vulnerability with dance floor energy, striving to evoke emotion while keeping the crowd moving.
