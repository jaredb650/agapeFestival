# Winter landing v1 (Sept 30 – Oct 30, 2026)

The single-screen Winter Edition landing (logo, countdown, Get tickets, scroll-locked
"lift to gallery" deck of 18 Sept photos) that replaced the September site, preserved before the
full winter redesign replaced it. The .tsx files have a .txt suffix to keep them out of the active build.
`GlitchLogo.tsx` is still in the app (the new hero uses it on desktop); `src/data/gallery.ts` and the
gallery photos remain in the app, unused.
